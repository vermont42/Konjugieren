# Choice questions to review (40)

Both sentences of every item have passed the machine check, which confirms each
conjugation against the conjugator. What it cannot judge is the choice itself:
whether a German speaker or writer in that setting would use the first sentence
over the second, whether the reason is true of it, and whether the English says
the same thing. The right sentence is listed first here; the app shows the two
in either order.
